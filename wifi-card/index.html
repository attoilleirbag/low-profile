Low Profile / Wi-Fi Card

Wi-Fi Card

Make a card guests can scan with their phone camera to join your Wi-Fi, without reading out a long password. Good for holiday rentals, offices and the fridge door. The password stays on this page and isn't saved anywhere.

Colour

The example values are made up. Replace them with your own.

Test it: point your phone camera at the code before you print.

Works with

iPhone camera (iOS 11+) and most Android phones (Android 10+) recognise Wi-Fi codes directly, with no app needed.

Private by design

The QR code is drawn by your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing is stored when you close the page.

A tip for hosts

Run a separate guest network on your router, so visitors never get the password to the one your own devices use.
Part of Low Profile. QR codes are generated with the open-source qrcode-generator library.
