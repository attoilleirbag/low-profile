Low Profile / Scrub

Scrub

Phone photos often carry the exact spot where they were taken, plus your phone model and the time. Drop photos here to see what they reveal, then save clean copies to share. Your photos never leave this device.

No photo handy?

What gets removed

Everything that isn't the picture itself: GPS coordinates, camera make and model, lens, date and time, editing software and embedded thumbnails.

How it works

Your browser redraws each photo onto a blank canvas and saves that, which keeps the pixels and drops the hidden data. Nothing is uploaded.

Worth knowing

Many social apps strip location on upload, but email, messaging apps sending "original quality", cloud links and marketplaces often keep it.
Part of Low Profile. iPhone HEIC photos open only in Safari; elsewhere, share them as "Most Compatible" (JPEG) first.
